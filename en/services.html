Diagnose · Design · Deliver · Verify

Work backwards
from what must work.

Services are organised around failure, change and operational consequence—not vendor categories. A small diagnostic engagement can stay small. A rebuild can extend from upstream path to the last device in the field.

Service map

Four kinds of intervention.

Choose the closest starting point. The actual scope is defined after the system and its constraints are visible.

01TroubleshootingSomething fails or behaves unpredictably 02PlanningA site is changing, growing or being created 03DeliveryThe architecture needs to become a working system 04Difficult connectivityInternet access is limited, unreliable or difficult to provide
01

Diagnose

Network troubleshooting

Symptoms are clues.
Not root causes.

Intermittent Wi‑Fi, failed roaming, congestion, disappearing devices and unstable uplinks often share causes outside the component that appears broken.

Observe

Measure the real environment, traffic patterns and failure timing before changing the system.

Map

Make topology, dependencies, RF conditions and upstream behaviour visible enough to reason about.

Isolate

Separate contributing conditions from the failure that actually drives the operational impact.

Recover

Apply the smallest defensible correction, then verify that the affected workflow works as intended.

Useful first materialA rough topology, device list, screenshots of errors and notes about when the problem appears. Imperfect information is fine.
02

Design

Network planning

Decisions before boxes.

New sites, renovations and inherited environments need a system model before a bill of materials. Coverage, capacity, segmentation, power, cabling and resilience are one design problem.

A

Constraints

Building fabric, landscape, power, cabling routes, budget, seasonality and the people who will operate it.

B

Workloads

Guests, staff, payments, surveillance, access, production and other systems that must not fail together.

C

Failure behaviour

Which paths can fail, what the system should do next and what a human needs to understand when it does.

D

Growth

Enough headroom and structure for the next real change, without buying capacity for purely hypothetical needs.

03

Build

Installation & rebuilds

Architecture made physical.

A clean design still has to survive cable paths, weather, power, mounting, labelling, change windows and the working day around it.

Direct work

Configuration, network hardware, wireless, field radios, validation and the system logic that holds it together.

Coordinated work

Trusted fibre, electrical, construction and other specialists when the project needs disciplines outside the core.

Acceptance testThe real workflow works, under the conditions it was designed for.
04

Connect

Internet anywhere

One outside path is not always a plan.

Mountain, rural and temporary sites may need fibre, mobile, satellite and radio paths to cooperate. The useful design is the one that understands their different failure modes.

FibreLTE / 5GSatellitePoint-to-pointMulti-WAN

Path diversity only helps when routing, monitoring, power and operational expectations are designed with it. “Four Internet connections” is not the same thing as resilient service.

Independent systems architecture

Small by design.
Serious by consequence.

AlpskiMedved.solutions is built around one systems architect with clear responsibility for the work. The scope expands when the problem calls for it.

The front door

Networks first.

Network architecture stays front and centre because it is the technical core: connectivity, routing, switching, wireless, segmentation, resilience and the field conditions that make them difficult.

Adjacent business systems belong when they share the same operational problem. They do not become a vague promise to “do all IT.”

Operating principles

02

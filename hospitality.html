Hospitality networks

Guest Wi‑Fi is
only one workload.

Payments, staff devices, cameras, access, reception, back office and guest expectations share the same physical environment. They should not share the same failure.

The operating system underneath the venue

Separate what should be separate.
Connect what must cooperate.

The useful hospitality network is quiet: guests get access, operations keep moving and staff do not need to understand the architecture to survive a busy service.

Privacy by restraint

A simple site.
No tracking layer.

The initial release is intentionally small: useful information and direct contact routes, without behavioural analytics or data-harvesting extras.

What this site does

Very little with your data.

This website does not use analytics, advertising technology, tracking cookies, submission forms or third-party embeds.

The project-brief builder runs locally in your browser. Your answers are not submitted to AlpskiMedved.solutions or stored by the site. Copying or printing a brief is an action on your own device.

If you choose an email or telephone link, your communication is handled by your own email or telephone service and is then processed only for the purpose of responding to your enquiry and carrying out any resulting work.

Site operation

Necessary infrastructure only.

01

No cookie banner

The site does not set analytics, advertising or preference cookies, so there is nothing optional to accept.

02

No hidden submission

There is no contact-form endpoint and the brief builder does not transmit its contents.

03

Standard server logs

The hosting and security infrastructure may temporarily process technical request data such as IP address, time, requested page and browser information to deliver and protect the site.

04

Direct questions

Use the approved public contact routes on the home page for privacy questions or requests concerning your communications.

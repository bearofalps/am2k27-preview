What makes a useful brief

Evidence beats certainty.

You do not need a diagnosis before the first conversation. Observations, constraints and operational consequences are more useful than a premature equipment list.

GOOD SIGNAL

“Payments fail when the terrace fills.”

It identifies a workload, a condition and a consequence.

GOOD SIGNAL

“The far building has no cable path.”

It exposes a physical constraint before a design is proposed.

STILL USEFUL

“We do not know what is installed.”

Uncertainty is an input to discovery, not a reason to guess.
